3. For each dedicated vendor account, verify the company customer, applicable agreement and recovery/access controls. Capture account identifiers privately without credentials. Paid upgrades, legally binding acceptance and Stripe account onboarding require their specific owner decisions/actions; this packet supplies no blanket authorization for them.
4. Prepare a resource-by-resource migration and rollback plan against actual deployed environments. Reconcile existing customer data, suppression, usage/trial state, retention holds and privacy outcomes before migration. Account creation alone does not cover old provider copies or prove deployed credential linkage.
5. Integration reviews and executes credential/configuration changes and any deployment. Keep existing resources inventoried until permitted cleanup is completed with evidence; no deletion or migration occurs in this task.
6. Close the customer agreement only after the actual first customer and operational/provider annexes are complete. Preserve both parties' acceptance. Close retention only with class-specific schedules, residual-copy limits and restore/erasure reconciliation evidence on the actual targets.
