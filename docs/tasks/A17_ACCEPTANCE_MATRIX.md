## Pending policy: how to record

The approved trial rule is one 14-day trial per business and billing owner, consumed only on authoritative Stripe trial start; unknown legacy history requires reconciliation. The approved Booster policy is 500/1,500 UTC calendar-month caps independent of invoice period, with no rollover, trial conversion continuity, reservations for unknown send outcome, and seven-day deferral eligibility. Tests should use frozen UTC time, including month boundary, and verify these contracts.

Do not accept a test result as approval for proposed changes. A18 still marks as pending: business-shared Reply safety ceiling and related customer copy; recovery-only grace/read access; scheduled downgrade timing and seat compatibility; and captured-versus-held visits after selected-location changes. Record a decision owner/date before assigning new expected behavior. Existing immediate Stripe plan-change behavior can be tested as observed, but it does not settle the proposed next-period downgrade contract.

## Evidence labels for final report

Use exactly these labels in status updates: `isolated tests`, `authenticated browser`, `provider test mode`, `production smoke`, and `external approval`. State `not run`, `blocked`, or `passed` per label and attach a sanitized receipt. Never aggregate them into one “acceptance passed” statement.
