## Proposed obligations

1. **Instructions and purpose.** The customer determines the purposes and lawful instructions for its recipient lists, visits, permission evidence, review requests and replies. Ornigami processes that data only to provide the agreed service and on documented instructions, including instructions about transfers, unless applicable law requires otherwise. Where permitted, Ornigami informs the customer of that legal requirement before processing. Ornigami informs the customer if it considers an instruction to infringe applicable data-protection law. Additional purposes or channels require a reviewed instruction change.
2. **Confidentiality and access.** Ornigami restricts access to authorized persons who need it for the service and are subject to confidentiality duties. Customer users must be scoped to the agreed workspace/business. Support exports and rights evidence are handled privately with restricted access; they are not committed to source control.
3. **Security.** Ornigami implements technical and organizational measures appropriate to the processing risks, as described in the completed security annex. The annex must distinguish implemented source controls from verified deployed controls and document target/access roles, transport protection, token/key custody, tenant scoping, incident handling, retention holds and restore restrictions. A code test is not evidence that the operational measure is deployed.
4. **Subprocessors.** Ornigami engages subprocessors only under the authorization option selected in the provider annex and places applicable processing obligations on them. For general authorization, complete the advance-notice channel, notice period, objection window and resolution procedure before acceptance. For specific authorization, list approved providers/services explicitly. No general approval is inferred from a public vendor list, and independent-controller vendor purposes must be distinguished.
5. **Rights assistance.** Ornigami assists the customer with access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability requests, taking account of the nature of processing. Recipient requests are routed securely to the customer controller, with documented instructions and deadlines. Identity and scope are checked proportionately; responses must protect other people's data. Self-service deletion remains disabled, and valid erasure requests are assessed independently of ordinary retention periods.
6. **Incidents and assessment assistance.** Ornigami notifies the customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting this processing and provides available information and updates needed for the customer's response. Ornigami assists, taking account of the nature of processing and information available, with security obligations, breach notifications, impact assessments and prior consultation where applicable. Complete operational escalation contacts and the incident procedure in the security annex.
7. **Information and audits.** Ornigami makes available information necessary to demonstrate compliance with the agreed processing obligations and allows/contributes to applicable audits, including inspections, under a completed proportionate audit procedure. That procedure must not make mandatory audit/rights obligations ineffective or disclose another customer's data.
8. **Return, deletion and retained exceptions.** At service end, follow the customer's documented return/delete choice, secure export recipient and delivery method. Document the handling period and assess any retained legal exceptions by data class, restricting further processing to the permitted purpose. Preserve only assessed minimum suppression, trial and unresolved-operation safeguards; do not reset sending eligibility. Explain provider and backup limits accurately, isolate remaining copies and reapply erasure/restriction before restored data can be used. No immediate all-copy deletion promise is made by this draft.
9. **Transfers and precedence.** Complete the processing locations, applicable safeguards and transfer assessment for the selected arrangement before acceptance. This draft alone does not authorize an international transfer. The executed agreement must specify its relationship to service terms and any applicable transfer clauses.
